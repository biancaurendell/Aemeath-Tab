## v1.0.0-aemeath.0

> Initial release of Aemeath's Tab (飞行雪绒标签页).

- Forked from Aemeath New Tab as the Aemeath customization branch.
- Aemeath customization layer (`AemeathLayer`): top-right music button, Meting player, lyric panel and playlist drawer, pixel pet, meteor background, click hearts.
- Aemeath settings page: music toggle and playlist URL, pet toggle and motion switch, meteor and click-heart toggles.
- Default wallpaper replaced with the Aemeath illustration; upstream wallpapers are no longer bundled.
- Soft, glassmorphic visual overrides.
